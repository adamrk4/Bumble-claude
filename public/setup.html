How to Get Your Bumble Token

We need your Bumble auth token to read your matches and send messages on your behalf. Use the free Proxyman iOS app to capture it — this takes about 2 minutes.

1

Install Proxyman on your iPhone

Search "Proxyman" in the App Store and install it (it's free).

2

Install the Proxyman certificate

Open Proxyman → tap "Certificate" → follow the steps to install and trust the certificate in Settings → General → VPN & Device Management.

3

Start capturing traffic

In Proxyman, tap the record button. Then switch to Bumble and do anything (open a chat, swipe, etc.).

4

Find the Authorization header

  • In Proxyman, find a request to am1.bumble.com
  • Tap on it → "Request" tab → Headers
  • Find the Authorization header (starts with Bearer )
  • Copy everything after "Bearer " — that's your token
5

Also copy the device ID (optional but recommended)

In the same request headers, look for x-pinguid or x-device-id and copy its value.

Token Expiry

Bumble tokens typically expire after a few days or when you log out of the app. If you see "Token expired" errors, just repeat this process to get a fresh token.
